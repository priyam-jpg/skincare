# Managing your store

Everything is managed in the admin dashboard at **yourdomain.ch/admin**. Log in with your admin email and password.

## Add a product

1. **Shop → Products → Create New**.
2. **Product name**, then on the **Photos & details** tab:
   - **Tagline** (e.g. "Hydrating essence") and **Size** (e.g. "150 ml").
   - **Photos**: add at least one. The **first photo** is the main image. The **second photo** appears when shoppers hover over the product card, which is a good place for a texture or "how it looks on skin" shot. Square photos work best. Fill in the short description of each photo when asked; it helps visually impaired shoppers and search engines.
   - **Description**, **How to use**, **Key ingredients**.
3. On the **Price & stock** tab:
   - Tick **Enable CHF price** and enter the price (including VAT).
   - **Inventory**: how many you have in stock.
   - **Compare-at price** (optional): the original price. If it is higher than the price, the shop shows "12% OFF" and the old price crossed out.
   - **Low-stock threshold**: at or below this number the product page shows "Only 3 left".
4. In the right-hand column: choose a **Badge** (New launch, Bestseller, Limited edition), **Categories** and **Related products** ("Pairs well with").
5. Click **Publish changes**. Until you publish, the product is a draft and shoppers can't see it. Changes are saved automatically as you type, but they only go live when you publish.

## Languages (German, French, Italian, English)

The shop is in four languages. On their first visit, shoppers pick one in a pop-up, and they can switch any time with the language button at the top of the page.

The buttons, menus, checkout and other shop text are already translated. **Your own text** (product names and descriptions, categories, homepage text) is written per language:

1. Open a product (or a category, or **Site → Homepage**).
2. Use the **language switcher at the top right of the edit screen** (it shows "English" by default) to choose Deutsch, Français or Italiano.
3. The translatable fields are now empty or show the English text. Type the translation and click **Publish changes**.

Price, stock, photos and settings are shared by all languages, so you only set them once. Anything you haven't translated yet shows in English, so the shop never shows a blank space.

The admin itself shows in your browser's language. To change it, click your account (bottom left) and pick a language.

## Stock

- Stock goes down **automatically** when an order is paid.
- At **0** the product shows **Sold out** and can't be added to the bag.
- Restocking: open the product, change **Inventory**, click **Publish changes**.
- The **Products** list shows the stock of every product, so sort by **Inventory** to see what's running low.

### Sizes or variants (optional)

For a product sold in several sizes: tick **Enable variants**, pick or create a variant type (e.g. "Size") with options ("30 ml", "50 ml"), and add a variant for each option with its own price and stock.

## Orders

**Orders → Orders** lists every paid order with the shopper, items, total and shipping address.

- New orders arrive as **Processing** (paid, needs packing).
- When you ship, open the order, change **Status** to **Completed** and save. The shopper sees "Shipped" on their order page.
- Refunds are made in Stripe (dashboard.stripe.com). Then set the order to **Refunded** here.

If email is set up, you get an email for each new order, and the shopper gets a confirmation.

## The homepage

**Site → Homepage** controls:

- **Announcement bar messages** (top pink strip, up to three).
- **Hero** headline and text.
- **"Most-loved formulas"**: pick the products. If you leave it empty, products tagged **Bestseller** appear.
- **Five-step routine**: each step's name, an optional Korean caption, a short note and the product shown next to it.
- **"Routines, boxed together"**: pick up to two set products. A set is an ordinary product with its own price and stock. Give it a compare-at price to show the saving.

"New to the shelf" always shows the eight most recently added products.

## Categories

**Shop → Categories**. Categories appear as filter buttons on the Shop page.

## Customers

**Customers → Customers & admins** lists every account. To give a colleague admin access, open their account (they first sign up on the shop), set **Roles** to **Admin**, and save. Remove the role to take access away.

Customers can sign up, save addresses, see their orders and reset their password from the shop. Shoppers can also check out as guests.

## Photos library

**Shop → Photos** holds every uploaded image. You can also upload directly from inside a product.
