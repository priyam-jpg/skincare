# Surface brief: K-beauty storefront homepage

Mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led (no image generation). Stack: Next.js App Router, plain CSS, Google Sans (user-pinned) plus Noto Sans KR for Hangul captions only. Theme: light. Palette (user, round 3): mostly white and black; red only for sale tags and the bag count, green only in Hangul captions. Photography supplied by the user: hero (close-up-people-getting-stronger-together.jpg, 6000x4000, white studio backdrop), herb oil bottle (herb-oil.png), portrait with white rose (portrait-rose.png).

Audience and job: shoppers (mostly mobile) finding skincare, trusting it, adding to cart. Proof available: none real; reviews/prices/certifications are labelled sample data in src/data/sample-content.ts. Stats band deliberately omitted: no verified numbers exist. The drawer-wall shop-by-concern section was removed at the user's request.

Unresolved: real brand name, currency, cart/checkout wiring, licence confirmation for the supplied photos.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The header and hero are one white field: the photo's white studio wall dissolves into the page so the transparent header, headline and four faces share a single surface. Refuses a boxed hero image under a solid nav bar.

OWN-WORLD: Pure white ground (#FFFFFF), near-black ink (#111111) with grey secondary (#595959), light grey bands (#F4F4F2), a black announcement bar and black footer. Primary actions are black; red (#A3362B) appears only as the Sale/New/Bestseller tag and bag count. Google Sans at weight 600 with tight negative tracking; Hangul captions in Noto Sans KR in green (#3F5F3A). Soft neutral shadows, generous spacing, thin rules.

STORY: Visitor sees real people with flowers on their skin and "Skincare that fits every skin", goes to bestsellers or a five-step routine, trusts via ingredient labels and a short brand story, and buys.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black announcement bar, then a transparent centred-logo header (nav left, tools right) over a white hero. Headline "Skincare that fits every skin." left, a black "Shop bestsellers" action and a ghost "Build your routine". The user's four-person photo sits right and bottom, masked to fade on its top and left edges into the white page. On phones the copy leads and the photo follows, fading up into the white.

FORM: Photo-dissolve split hero (white-studio blend) with the numbered routine as the page's ordered device. Previously assigned form (Herbal Drawer Wall, seed:8c7347de4b40270a) retired by the user's decision to remove that section; raises kept: strict numbered margin column in the routine (Washi Fold).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
