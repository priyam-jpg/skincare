# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 + Payload CMS 3 (admin at /admin) + Postgres, hosted on Render. Stripe (TWINT, cards, Apple/Google Pay) in CHF; Cloudinary for uploaded photos. See docs/DEPLOY.md.

## Users

Shoppers browsing a Korean-skincare (K-beauty) online store, mostly on phones: people building or refining a multi-step routine who want to know which product fits their skin, whether it is trustworthy (ingredients, certifications), and what to buy first.

## Product Purpose

A full online store for a Swiss-based Korean skincare brand, in German, French, Italian and English (first-visit language prompt, header picker): shop, product pages, bag, checkout, customer accounts and an admin where the client manages products, stock, orders and the homepage. Success: a visitor understands the brand's K-beauty point of view in seconds, finds a bestseller or a starter routine, and adds to cart. The template owner swaps name, copy and imagery to launch.

## Positioning

K-beauty routine thinking (layered, gentle, ingredient-led) presented as a soft, rounded white, black and light-pink storefront, structured like the D2C skincare homepages the user referenced (announcement bar, transparent-header photo hero, trust strip, product carousels, reviews).

## Operating Context

Real store: cart, checkout, inventory, accounts and order management work end to end. Not yet built: shipping rates, landing-page builder, reviews system, newsletter integration, translated emails.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: homepage, shop (search, categories, sort), product pages, bag drawer, checkout, order confirmation, login/sign-up/password reset, account, addresses, order history. Homepage sections: announcement bar, transparent-header photo hero, trust strip, most-loved carousel, five-step routine, routine sets, ingredient labels, new-arrivals carousel, brand story, reviews, footer. A stats band and a shop-by-concern section were deliberately left out (no verified numbers; the user removed the concern section).
- Placeholder brand name "Your Brand" (undecided; owner supplies).
- Typeface pinned by user: Google Sans.
- Light theme pinned by user; palette is white and black with light pink (#FFE9F0 to #FBD4E0) as the soft third colour and one deeper rose (#B32E5C) for small marks and Hangul captions. Shapes are rounded and a little playful (pill buttons, bubble step numbers).
- The catalogue is the client's real range: 45 genuine Korean products (skincare, hair & scalp, body, lip care, sets), imported from their German and French product posters. Each product has a square photo cut from its poster on a soft background, plus the full poster shown whole. Prices (CHF) are developer placeholders awaiting the client's price list. Three user-supplied photos are in use (hero, ingredients background, story portrait); licences are unconfirmed.
- Reference screenshots (clayco.in) guide section structure only; no copying of branding, copy, logos or imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real customer reviews, ratings, sales figures, awards or certifications exist. All such content in the template is synthetic placeholder and must be labelled/replaced by the owner. Do not present invented numbers as real.

## Product Principles

1. Routine-first: shoppers are guided by steps and skin need, not by catalog.
2. Trust is shown with specifics (ingredients, concentrations, certifications) never with vague superlatives.
3. Mobile-first; the thumb is the primary input.
4. Every claim and number is replaceable placeholder data, clearly flagged.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, visible focus, keyboard-operable carousels with pause controls, reduced-motion respected.
